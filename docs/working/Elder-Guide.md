# Schedule a Membership Meeting
## Elder Guide

This guide covers the three things you'll need to do in the system: log in, set your availability, and see your upcoming meetings. That's it.

---

## 1. Logging In

1. Open your web browser and go to the scheduling website (ask your admin for the link if you don't have it — during testing it is `elder.techfoundry360.com/manage`).
2. Enter the shared PIN when asked. Your admin will give you this PIN.
3. Find your name in the list of elders.

You do not need a username or password — just the PIN and your name.

---

## 2. Setting Your Availability

This tells the system when members can book a meeting with you.

1. After logging in and selecting your name, look for **your availability** or **your schedule**.
2. Choose the day(s) and time(s) you're willing to meet with members.
   - You can pick more than one day and time.
   - We recommend picking a regular time each week (for example, "Sundays at 9:00 a.m.") so it's predictable — but you're not limited to Sundays or church service times.
3. Save your changes.

**If your availability changes** (vacation, a scheduling conflict, etc.), come back and update it. If a member has already booked a meeting with you during a time you can no longer make, contact them directly to reschedule — the system does not do this automatically for a change you make after the booking exists.

---

## 3. Viewing Your Upcoming Meetings

1. After logging in, look for **your upcoming meetings** or **appointments**.
2. This will show you who you're meeting with, along with the date and time.
3. You'll also get an email confirmation whenever a member books a meeting with you — you don't have to check the website to know a meeting was booked.

---

## Good to Know

- **Meeting location:** Meetings are set up at your campus by default. You're welcome to call the member and change the meeting place if that works better for both of you.
- **Only touch your own schedule.** You'll see your name in a list — please don't change availability for other elders. If something needs fixing for someone else, let your admin know.

---

## Need Help?

If something isn't working, or you're not sure how to do something in this guide, contact **engagement@gocoastal.org**.
